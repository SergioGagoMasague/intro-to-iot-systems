Appendix E — Setting Up a Teaching Lab

This appendix is for instructors and lab managers who want to run the course, or a course like it. It describes what a room needs so that every team can build, solder, program and test its kit safely. Quantities assume a section of about 30 students working in teams of two or three, with up to 10 people soldering at once.

E.1 The room

E.2 Shared equipment (per 10 soldering stations)

In Fall 2026 this shared equipment cost about $1,900 for 10 stations, and kit parts for 36 new kits about $2,000.

E.3 Before the first lab

  • Kits: count, label and test every kit; flash a kit-check sketch; pre-solder or pre-provision anything students should not do themselves (for example, locking secure elements).
  • Network: test that 30 boards can join the room network and reach your broker or server at the same time.
  • Safety briefing: present the soldering procedure, show the safety station and require safety glasses.
  • Staffing: plan at least one teaching assistant per 10 students during soldering.
  • Kit policy: decide whether kits are returned at the end of the term or can be bought, and how damaged parts are replaced.

E.4 Running costs

Budget each term for consumables (solder, flux, wire, resistors and LEDs), replacement parts (an ESP32 or a sensor per few kits is typical), and spare tips for the soldering stations.
