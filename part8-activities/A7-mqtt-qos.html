Activity 7: MQTT Command Center and Picking the QoS

Time: about 30 minutes · Format: team of 2–3 with the kit · Hardware: kit Stage 1 and a classroom Wi-Fi network with an MQTT broker · Chapter: 12  Application Protocols: HTTP, CoAP, MQTT and More

Goal

Connect the board to a classroom MQTT broker. It publishes potentiometer telemetry to a team topic and subscribes to a class-wide command topic, so that one message from the instructor changes every kit in the room. Then decide which QoS, retain and Last Will settings fit four real situations.

MQTT delivery options.
Term Meaning
QoS 0 At most once: no acknowledgment; may be lost
QoS 1 At least once: resent until acknowledged; may be duplicated
QoS 2 Exactly once: a four-step handshake, at extra cost
Retained The broker keeps the last message on a topic and gives it to new subscribers
Last Will (LWT) A message the broker publishes for a client that disconnects unexpectedly

Part A: the command center

Set the Wi-Fi values, MQTT_HOST, MQTT_PORT (1883) and MQTT_TOPIC_BASE in include/secrets.h, and your TEAM_ID.

Activity 7 TODOs.
TODO Task Hint
1 Point the client at the broker and register the callback mqttClient.setServer(...), mqttClient.setCallback(onMqttMessage)
2 Subscribe to the command topic after connecting Keep the Boolean result of subscribe()
3 Publish telemetry on Button 1 Topic <base>/team/<TEAM_ID>/telemetry; payload TEAM=<team>;SEQ=<n>;SENSOR=<percent>
4 Act on commands ALERT → activateAlert(); NORMAL → restoreNormalMode() (case-sensitive)

When the instructor publishes ALERT, every connected kit shows an alert and changes its LEDs; NORMAL restores them.

Part B: pick the QoS

The starter’s library publishes at QoS 0, so this part is a design exercise. For each situation choose a QoS level, decide whether the message is retained, and justify it in one sentence. Then choose one situation where a Last Will helps and write its topic and payload.

  1. A greenhouse sensor publishes temperature every 10 seconds to a live dashboard.
  2. A phone app sends an “unlock” command to a smart door lock.
  3. A smart electricity meter reports a daily billing reading that must be counted exactly once.
  4. A dashboard must show whether each device is online, including devices that just lost power.

Extension (optional)

On Button 2, publish HELLO from team <TEAM_ID> to <base>/team/<TEAM_ID>/chat. Would other teams receive it? What would they need to subscribe to? Try a wildcard (+ or #).

Troubleshooting

  • Broker connection fails: check the host, port and that the board is on the class network; note the state code printed.
  • Your messages do not appear: the topic base must match exactly.
  • ALERT does nothing: check the subscription result and the string comparison.

Questions

  1. Situations 1 and 2: which QoS and retain setting did you choose for each, and why?
  2. Situation 3: which QoS did you choose? Could QoS 1 work, and what would the receiver have to do?
  3. Situation 4: write the Last Will topic and payload, and say whether it should be retained.
  4. Why could the instructor alert every kit with one publish, without knowing how many were connected?
